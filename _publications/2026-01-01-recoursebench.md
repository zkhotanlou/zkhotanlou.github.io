Co-developed a scalable Python benchmarking platform integrating 22 heterogeneous ML methods across 137 method, dataset, and model configurations. Designed standardized interfaces for consistent evaluation of heterogeneous methods across ML frameworks, and built automated CI and reproducibility tests to validate execution reliability, output consistency, and fidelity to reported results.
